Generate a Monthly Master Document for [COMPANY/TREND] for [MONTH YEAR] and create it as a new page in Analyst Labs.

STEP 1 — Identify source documents for the month:
- All Weekly Documents for this company/trend during [MONTH]
- All Sector Briefs that include this company/trend during [MONTH]
- All Thesis Reviews for this company during [MONTH]
- All Signals in Analyst Labs tagged to this company during [MONTH]
- All Meeting Notes linked to this company during [MONTH]
- Tacit Knowledge entries linked to this company created during [MONTH]
- Company Metrics data from [MONTH]

STEP 2 — Create page in Analyst Labs using the Monthly Master Document template with Type = "Monthly Master Document". Link to company and trend.

STEP 3 — Fill the template sections:

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: 5 bullets capturing the most important developments of the month and their thesis implications.

WHAT CHANGED THIS MONTH: Organized by category (Financial updates, CEO statements, Product reveals, Competitive moves, Regulatory, etc.)

WHY IT MATTERS: Connect changes to the investment thesis. Not just what happened — why it matters for our position.

DECISION / STANCE UPDATE: Any changes in conviction, allocation, or research status during the month.

EXPECTED vs ACTUAL (MANDATORY):
Table with columns: What We Expected | What Actually Happened | Delta (🟢/🔴/🟡) | Thesis Implication
Pull expectations from:
- Prior month's Monthly Master ("Expectations for next month" section)
- Thesis Review predictions
- Sector Brief open questions
Net assessment: X of Y expectations met. Pattern analysis. Calibration notes.
Expectations for next month: 3-5 specific, falsifiable predictions to contrast next month.

COMPETITIVE / MARKET STRUCTURE UPDATE: What competitors did, market shifts, pricing changes, regulatory moves.

THESIS STATUS: Current thesis (from Thesis Review) + conviction + key question for next month.

FOUNDER TAKEAWAYS: Compiled from Sector Brief calls and any founder sessions during the month.

ANALYST TAKEAWAYS: Compiled per analyst from Weekly Docs, Sector Briefs, and signals.

CATALYST & TIMELINE MAP: Next 30/60/90 day catalysts with dates.

PATTERN CHAINING: Links to prior Monthly Master + reinforcing/contradicting signals + cross-month connections.

NEXT STEPS: Action items with owners.

QUALITY STANDARDS:
• Expected vs Actual is MANDATORY and must have real data, not placeholders
• Every claim must have a clickable source link
• Include founder language verbatim when available
• The Executive Summary must be actionable, not descriptive
• Expectations for next month must be specific and falsifiable
• Connect to the prior Monthly Master for continuity
